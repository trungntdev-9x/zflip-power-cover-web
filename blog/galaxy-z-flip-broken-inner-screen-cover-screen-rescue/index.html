1. The Foldable Reality: When the Inner Screen Goes Black

It is the nightmare scenario every folding phone owner dreads. You open your Samsung Galaxy Z Flip, and suddenly:

  • The inner display stays completely pitch black ("Black Screen of Death").
  • A violent cluster of green or purple vertical lines flares across the hinge.
  • The top half of the screen works, but the bottom half is dead to touch input.
  • Black ink bleed spreads outward from the folding crease.

The Cost Reality Check

An official out-of-warranty screen replacement from Samsung authorized centers costs between $300 and $420 USD. In many instances, Samsung technicians deny warranty claims by pointing to minor scuffs on the hinge frame as evidence of "accidental drops," leaving users stranded without their primary phone, bank authenticators, or WhatsApp backups.

Before you rush into an expensive repair or purchase a replacement phone, there is an incredible piece of hardware engineering you can leverage: Your 3.4-inch outer Cover Screen (Flex Window) is completely independent and 100% functional.

2. Understanding Android Multi-Display: Display 0 vs. Display 1

Internally, Android treats the Galaxy Z Flip as a dual-display workstation:

  • Display ID 0: The internal 6.7-inch folding AMOLED panel (22:9 aspect ratio, 1080x2640).
  • Display ID 1: The external 3.4-inch Super AMOLED Flex Window (720x748 resolution).

When your inner screen breaks, the phone's processor (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 / Gen 3), RAM, modem, and primary operating system (Display 0) continue to run normally in the background. However, Samsung's default One UI software and Good Lock MultiStar restrict the cover screen to a tiny curated whitelist of basic widgets. You cannot open system settings, access banking apps, authenticate Google prompts, or access your file system.

This is where Z Flip Cover Power fundamentally changes the game.

4. The Game-Changer: Two-Way Hardware Touch Injection

Most screen mirroring apps are view-only: you can see what is happening, but you cannot interact with it. If your inner screen is unresponsive, a view-only mirror is useless.

Z Flip Cover Power solves this by coupling screen projection with True Shizuku Touch Injection (implemented in ShizukuInputUtil.kt):

How Shizuku Touch Injection Works

When you touch, swipe, pinch, or type on the external cover screen, Cover Mirror captures the exact touch coordinates, maps them to the primary screen dimensions, and sends them through Android's low-level service manager directly into IInputManager.injectInputEvent.

This means your physical taps on the outer glass are registered by the Android kernel as legitimate physical touches on the main screen. You can:

  1. Unlock your phone using your pattern, PIN, or fingerprint.
  2. Type on full on-screen keyboards (Gboard / Samsung Keyboard).
  3. Swipe down the full One UI notification shade and quick settings.
  4. Open any banking, social, or communication app without restriction.
  5. Navigate with gestures (Back, Home, Recents).

Accessibility Gesture Fallback

If you have not configured Shizuku yet, Cover Power includes an automatic fallback through an Android Accessibility Gesture Dispatcher, enabling basic clicks, long-presses, and scrolling swipes out of the box!

5. The 2:3 Aspect Ratio Tweak (Eliminating Letterbox Bars)

Here is a problem unique to folding phones: The Galaxy Z Flip inner display is unusually tall and narrow (22:9 ratio). If you mirror a 22:9 screen onto a nearly square 3.4-inch cover screen (approx. 1:1 ratio), the picture becomes tiny, flanked by massive black borders on the left and right.

Z Flip Cover Power solves this with a built-in Shizuku ADB Preset:

ADB Preset: 2:3 Aspect Ratio for Cover Mirror
wm size 1080x1600 && wm density 400

What this command accomplishes:

  • Forces Android's primary rendering viewport into a standard 2:3 aspect ratio (1080x1600) with a comfortable 400 DPI.
  • The mirrored projection instantly fills the Flex Window edge-to-edge.
  • Icons, text, buttons, and keyboard keys become large, legible, and easy to press with your thumb.
  • Whenever you want to restore original factory settings, the app has a 1-tap Reset Preset:
ADB Preset: Factory Screen Reset
wm size reset && wm density reset

7. Step-by-Step Emergency Setup Guide

Here is how to set up Cover Mirror depending on the current condition of your device:

Scenario A: Inner touch still partially responds OR you already have Shizuku installed

  1. Download and open Z Flip Cover Power from Google Play.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Shizuku ADB Presets.
  3. Enable the switch: "Projection with Shizuku". (This pre-grants screen capture rights via AppOps so you never need to confirm on the broken screen).
  4. Tap Run on the preset: Set Inner Screen to 2:3 Aspect Ratio.
  5. Close your phone. On your cover screen, tap the Mirror quick toggle. You are now controlling your entire phone!

Scenario B: Inner screen is 100% black / unresponsive

  1. Connect a standard USB mouse to your Z Flip using a USB-C to USB-A adapter. A mouse cursor will instantly appear on your screen (even if inner display is black, you can see it if mirrored, or use your PC).
  2. Alternatively, connect your phone to a computer via USB cable and run:

8. Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cover Mirror require root access?

No. Z Flip Cover Power uses official Android MediaProjection APIs and Shizuku over wireless debugging. Zero root privileges and zero bootloader unlocking are required. Your Knox security and Samsung Pay status remain intact.

Does mirroring drain my battery quickly?

No. When Cover Mirror is active, the broken inner display backlight is powered down, consuming virtually zero power. The app's rendering loop enters deep standby the millisecond the cover screen turns off or is placed in your pocket.

Can I answer calls and reply to WhatsApp chats?

Yes, completely. With full two-way touch injection, incoming call buttons, WhatsApp keyboards, bank authenticators, and camera viewfinders function exactly as they would on the main screen.
