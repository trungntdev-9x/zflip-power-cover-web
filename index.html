Privacy Policy & Transparency

Last updated: September 2026

1. Absolute Local Privacy: ZFlip Cover Power does not transmit any user data over the network. The app functions 100% offline.

2. Permissions Usage:
• ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Used strictly to detect cover screen fold events and launch selected apps into the secondary display target.
• QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: Used solely to display the list of your installed apps inside the cover launcher widget so you can pick which apps to pin.

3. No Data Storage: No personal logs, keystrokes, or screen contents are ever stored or recorded.
